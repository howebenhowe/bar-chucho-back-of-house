The nicks take it!

---

We wept, we drummed on shaker tins. You wept, you drummed on the bart-top. Then hundreds of you hit the street, milled and mingled with the Mr. Fong's crew, the roar of glory was raised into the belly of the Manhattan Bridge. Someone got stuck driving thrugh Market &amp; Madison, they seemed mad pumping their fist out the window--no wait, it's just terrible joy. A dozen of y'all disappeared with open tabs into the back of a NYC Parks Maintenance pickup truck, and with your beery encouragement, it went swaying and honking up Bowery. We were glad to see them come back to us, and in one piece. We're always glad to see you coming back.

With love and pride,
Chucho
