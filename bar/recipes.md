## cilantro syrup

TODO: insert table with estimates at least for amounts
(cilantro bunch count, mass, yield)

1. take cilantro and rip off the stem where no leaves grow
1. weigh cilantro and add 2 x weight in water
1. blend thoroughly in vitamix (few drops of pectinex optional)
1. strain through chinois, then through superbag or coffee filter
1. weigh then add 1 x liquid weight in white sugar
1. add 0.5% ascorbic acid (.005 x liquid weight)
1. add 2% salt (.02 x liquid weight)
1. stir sugar and salt into cold cilantro juice until dissolved

## meth syrup

| ingredient         | amount |
| :----------------- | -----: |
| methylcelulose f50 |    9 g |
| boiling water      |  300 g |
| cold water         |  600 g |
| sugar              |  900 g |

1. dissolve methylcelulose into boiling water and let rest 10 minutes
1. immersion-blend at lowest speed just cold water in bain,
   slowly add methycel solution, minimize aeration
1. blend for several minutes
1. place in the freezer for (x) min to dissipate foam
1. skim persistent foam with spoon
1. add sugar and gently whisk until dissolved
