# DOH checklist

## undersink & sani bucket

- Remove the towel bucket from under the service sink and put towels in the laundry in the kitchen. 
- Remove busbin. **Leave if kitchen is inspected first, then move to kitchen during the transition in inspection. 
- Red sani bucket must be filled & full. 
 
## towels

- Remove all hip & butt towels from your person. 
- All towels should be neatly folded and not laying on an open bar. 
- Remove any dirty towels or polish rags and put in the laundry basket in the kitchen. 
 
## garnish & fruit bowl

- Throw away all the garnish in metal containers & fruit bowls. 
- Wash both containers and bowls in the washer for storage. 
- Throw away Chucho by register

## uni prep & kegs

- Throw away all uni prep in the fridge. 
- Put the uni keg into a recycling bin (with top) to be later retrieved. 
- Put carajillo keg in fridge labeled with date. 
- Remove and dump hotel pan with water & ice. Pan will go back to dish pit. 

## handwash sink

- Remove catch-all basket from hand sink. Clean out sink and burn any remaining ice. 
- Check the hand soap next to the sink is full.
- Make sure c-folds are stocked.

## labels & expirations

- Make sure all items are labeled AND dated. No exceptions!
	- Inside fridge: Prep, olives, citrus, batch jugs, etc.
	- Well: All citrus, batches, citrus in beer fridge. 
	- Throw away any prep older then 7 days. 
- Label slushie machine with current date near the hopper on top. 

## ice well

- Make sure ice well lid remains closed. 
- Both scoops should be present in holders and holders are dry inside. 

## service sink

- Catch-all should be cleared of all debris and ice burnt. 
- Put clean catch-all into dish bin. 

## glassware & polishing

- Bottom rack with dirty glasses should go immediately into the washer and the shelf cleaned. 
- Ensure that all glassware on back bar is properly hung or placed on the black liners upside down. 
- Avoid polishing while DOH is in house. If needed, always use two rags to polish so no bare hands are touching glasses. 
- Dirty glassware should have garnish, straws, etc. go right into the trash can. Avoid allowing dirty glassware to stack up on barmats at the service station. 

## beer fridge

- Ensure that all citrus is properly labeled. 
- Throw away any citrus that has been peeled at all. 
- Remove any personal items (drink, food, etc). 
- Fridge just be only whole citrus on bottom, juiced citrus, and packaged drinks. 

## prep fridge

- Throw away any personal items (food, snacks, beverages, etc)
- All items should be neatly organized and in a row with a clear FIFO order.
- Check bottom shelf for stickiness. 
- Please wipe down with a towel of hot water and sanitizer if sticky.
